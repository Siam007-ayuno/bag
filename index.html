3-Phase Strategic Enterprise Execution Plan

A phased operational roadmap minimizes initial capital risk while creating a clear trajectory toward scalable private-label brand ownership.

Phase 1 (Months 1 - 3)

Proof of Concept & Market Testing

  • ✔ Sourcing: Domestic wholesale from Chawkbazar (Modina Ashik Tower) & corporate importers (The Patchee BD). Minimal upfront capital.
  • ✔ Legal: E-Trade license acquisition (DNCC/DSCC) & MFS bKash Merchant account set up.
  • ✔ Marketing: White background catalog photography (CK Studio/Color Experts BD) + basic Meta interest targeting.
  • ✔ Logistics: Onboard with Steadfast or Pathao Courier for COD.
Phase 2 (Months 4 - 9)

Market Penetration & Systematization

  • ✔ Marketing: Layered Meta Ads targeting (Females 25-45 + "Engaged Shoppers" AND "Handbags").
  • ✔ Automation: Integrate AI Sales Bot (Sellzy) for automated 24/7 Bangla Messenger replies & order booking.
  • ✔ Packaging: Shift to custom 3-ply corrugated mailers (JobaidaPack/WrapUp BD) and printed dust bags (100 MOQ).
  • ✔ Optimization: Active phone verification to drop RTO under 8%.
Phase 3 (Months 10+)

Private Labeling & Margin Expansion

  • ✔ Manufacturing: Partner with local OEM factories (NNT Bag Factory or BLJ Bangladesh for 100 pcs MOQ genuine leather).
  • ✔ Direct Import: Utilize door-to-door C&F forwarders for unique Guangzhou fast-fashion items.
  • ✔ Quality Control: Deploy 3rd party preshipment inspection protocols (SGS/Tetra Inspection).
  • ✔ E-Commerce: Launch standalone Shopify web portal backed by DBID certification.
