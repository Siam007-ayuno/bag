03 — The numbers that matter

Import duty & landed cost

Bangladesh protects its own leather and bag industry with a steep import tax. Any bag imported under HS code 4202.21 (leather) or 4202.22 (synthetic/PU/textile) is taxed almost double its port value.

What makes up the 96.1% tax

ComponentRateNotes
Customs Duty (CD)25.0%Base import duty
Supplementary Duty (SD)20.0%Protective duty on this category
Value Added Tax (VAT)15.0%Standard VAT
Advance Tax (AT)7.5%Can vary by fiscal year (sometimes 3%)
Advance Income Tax (AIT)5.0%Credited against income tax later
Regulatory Duty (RD)5.0%Applied on top of CD
Total Tax Incidence96.1%Applied to CIF value, not just item price

Tax is charged on the CIF value — item cost plus freight plus insurance. A $10 bag with $2 shipping becomes a $12 taxable base, adding roughly $11.53 in duty before it clears the port.

05 — Last mile

Delivery & cash-on-delivery risk

Because most orders are paid on delivery, your courier is effectively your cashier. Pricing is similar across the three national couriers — the real difference is in refused-delivery (RTO) rates.

Pathao Fastest in Dhaka

  • Inside Dhaka: 60–70 BDT
  • Outside Dhaka: 100–130 BDT
  • COD fee: 0.5% Dhaka / 1% outside
  • Payout: 1–2 business days

Steadfast Merchant favorite

  • Inside Dhaka: 55–65 BDT
  • Outside Dhaka: 100–120 BDT
  • COD fee: 1% flat
  • Payout: 2–3 business days

RedX Widest coverage

  • Inside Dhaka: 65–75 BDT
  • Outside Dhaka: 90–120 BDT
  • COD fee: 1% (occasional 0% promos)
  • Payout: 1–3 business days

06 — Staying compliant

Legal registration & payments

F-commerce is now formally regulated. These five items cover trade licensing, digital ID, delivery-time rules, and how to legally fund Meta ad spend.

0 of 5 completed

E-trade license (DNCC / DSCC)
Apply online via erevenue.dncc.gov.bd or erevenue.dscc.gov.bd. Base fee ~2,000 BDT + 15% VAT + signboard tax (80 BDT/sq ft). Annual renewal runs roughly 2,800–4,174 BDT.
Digital Business ID (DBID)
Mandatory registration with the Ministry of Commerce for e-commerce/F-commerce sellers. Display the DBID number on your storefront or Facebook page.
2021 Digital Commerce Guidelines
Terms and refund policy must be in Bangla. Hand over to courier within 48 hours. Max delivery: 5 days same-city, 10 days inter-district. Refund out-of-stock orders within 72 hours.
Dual-currency card & passport endorsement
Needed to pay for Meta ads. Passport endorsement against your travel quota is capped at 300 BDT by Bangladesh Bank.
15% VAT on ad spend
NBR charges 15% VAT automatically on Facebook/Meta ad payments through your bank. Build this into your customer acquisition cost.

Getting paid

A registered bKash Merchant Account is worth setting up early rather than using a personal account — it gives you QR codes, a merchant app, and settlement fees around 1.15–1.35%, and it signals legitimacy to customers and banks alike.

07 — Putting it together

A three-phase path

The lowest-risk way to build this business is to start cheap and local, then reinvest once you know what sells.

1
Months 1–3
Proof of concept
  • Sourcing: Buy small batches from Chawkbazar (Modina Ashik Tower) or a corporate supplier like The Patchee BD.
  • Legal: Get your DNCC/DSCC trade license and set up a bKash merchant account.
  • Photos: Basic white-background product shots — this matters more than most people expect.
  • Delivery: Onboard with Steadfast or Pathao for COD.
2
Months 4–9
Systematize
  • Ads: Layer Meta targeting — e.g. women 25–45 combined with "engaged shoppers."
  • Response time: Automate Messenger replies so leads aren't lost overnight.
  • Packaging: Move to custom mailer boxes and printed dust bags.
  • RTO control: Phone-verify orders to keep refusal rate under 8%.
3
Month 10+
Scale into your own brand
  • Manufacturing: Partner with an OEM factory (NNT Bag Factory, or BLJ for leather at 100-pc MOQ) for your own designs.
  • Import: Use a door-to-door freight forwarder for unique items from Guangzhou.
  • Quality control: Use a third-party inspector (SGS, Tetra Inspection) before large orders ship.
